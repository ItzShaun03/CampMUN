01 / THE IDEA

A forum for conviction.

When young people learn to disagree with care, they gain the power to shape what comes next.

“The future belongs to those who can turn a room of differences into a direction.”

Conversation is where change starts.

CampMUN is a space for future leaders to listen closely, speak precisely and build consensus across difference. It gives delegates the rigour of procedure and the freedom to think beyond easy answers.

Through considered debate, research and diplomacy, students meet the questions shaping our world — and one another.

THE HOST SCHOOL

A legacy of forming character.

Campion School was founded on 20 January 1943 by the Society of Jesus, with Fr. Joseph Savall S.J. as its founding principal. The school began at 45 New Marine Lines with 23 pupils and moved to Wellesley House, Cooperage Road, in 1948.

Its Ignatian tradition sees education as the formation of the whole person: intellectual competence, a reflective conscience and compassionate service. CampMUN is an extension of that tradition — a place to think rigorously, engage respectfully and act responsibly.
